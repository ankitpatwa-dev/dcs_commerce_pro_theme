================
DCS Commerce Pro
================

Premium eCommerce theme for Odoo 18 by `DevsCodeSpace <https://devscodespace.com/>`_.

Installation
============

1. Copy ``theme_dcs_ecommerce_pro`` and ``dcs_ecommerce_pro`` into your addons path.
2. Update the Apps list.
3. Open your website, click **Edit**, open the **Theme** tab, click **Switch Theme**
   and choose **DCS Commerce Pro**. eCommerce is installed if needed.

Configuration
=============

* **Palette and fonts**: editor, **Theme** tab. Three palettes are included
  (Shelf, Harbor, Grove) and three fonts (Outfit, DM Sans, Manrope).
* **Header and footer**: click the header or footer in edit mode, then
  **Customize > Template**. The theme selects *DCS Storefront* for both; Odoo's
  own templates remain available.
* **Announcement bar, footer texts**: click the text in edit mode and type.
* **Social links**: Website > Configuration > Settings, social media fields.
* **Blocks**: **Blocks** tab, group **DCS Commerce** (Shelf Hero).

Multi-website
=============

Only websites that select the theme are changed. On other websites of the same
database, the theme's views, fonts and styles are not loaded.

Privacy
=======

The theme stores no visitor data, adds no tracking and sends nothing to
DevsCodeSpace or third parties. Fonts are served from your Odoo server.

Compatibility
=============

Odoo 18.0 Community. Enterprise has not been tested.
