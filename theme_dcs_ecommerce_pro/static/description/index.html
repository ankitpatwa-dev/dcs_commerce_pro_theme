Odoo 18 eCommerce theme

DCS Commerce Pro

A storefront theme that puts your products first: a shopping header with live product search, product imagery that blends into the page, and palettes made for retail. Built on Odoo's own website builder, so your team edits it the way they already know.

By DevsCodeSpace

Why stores choose it

A header made for shopping

Announcement bar, a wide product search with images and prices as you type, account and cart within reach, and the menu on its own row.

Products stand out

Product photos sit in a tinted image well. White studio backgrounds blend into it, so pictures look consistent without editing.

Nothing new to learn

Colors, fonts, buttons, header and footer are set from the editor's Theme and Customize tabs. Blocks drag and drop like any Odoo block.

Your checkout stays Odoo's

Prices, pricelists, taxes, variants, cart and payment work exactly as in standard eCommerce. The theme changes how the store looks, not how it sells.

What is included

  • DCS Storefront header: announcement bar, logo, product search with live suggestions, account, cart, menu row, language selector and call-to-action. Odoo's mobile header with cart on phones.
  • DCS Storefront footer: service strip (delivery, returns, payment, help), shop, account and contact links, social links, copyright.
  • Shelf Hero block: headline, two buttons and a product photo in an image well with a tag-shaped label. Uses Odoo's standard background, color, spacing and animation options.
  • Three color palettes: Shelf (general retail and fashion), Harbor (electronics), Grove (beauty, wellness, home).
  • Fonts included: Outfit, DM Sans and Manrope are served from your own Odoo server. No request to Google Fonts.
  • Per website: on a multi-website database, only the websites that select the theme change. Others keep their own design.
  • Visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.

Getting started

  1. Copy theme_dcs_ecommerce_pro and dcs_ecommerce_pro into your addons path and update the Apps list.
  2. Open your website, click Edit, go to the Theme tab, click Switch Theme and choose DCS Commerce Pro. eCommerce is installed automatically if it is not there yet.
  3. Click Edit on your website. Change the announcement text, logo and footer text in place, pick a palette in the Theme tab, and add the Shelf Hero block from the DCS Commerce group.
  4. Set your social network links in Website > Configuration > Settings so the footer icons point to your pages.

Compatibility

Odoo 18.0 Community, tested on a clean database. Requires Website and eCommerce. Enterprise has not been tested yet. The theme does not change checkout, payment, tax or stock logic.

Privacy

The theme stores no visitor data, adds no tracking and sends nothing to DevsCodeSpace or third parties. Fonts and images load from your own server.
